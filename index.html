📖 The Midnight Signal

Read the six rebuilt chapters of this original Spider-Verse-inspired story.

CHAPTER ONE: THE SIGNAL

At 11:59 p.m., every screen in the city went black. Every screen except the one in Kai's bedroom.

A blue symbol appeared on the glass: a spider surrounded by a circle of stars. Then came a message.

IF YOU CAN READ THIS, YOUR UNIVERSE HAS BEEN FOUND.

CHAPTER TWO: THE OTHER SIDE

The symbol led Kai to an abandoned station beneath the city. Behind a locked door, the air shimmered like sunlight on water.

Someone stepped through. They wore a familiar mask, but the colours were different.

“You have to listen,” the stranger said. “Your world isn't the only one in danger.”

CHAPTER THREE: THE MISSING PIECE

The stranger called herself Nova. She came from a universe where the midnight signal had already swallowed the stars.

Kai wanted proof. Nova showed a photograph of the city, its skyline broken by a glowing crack in the sky.

In the corner of the photograph stood Kai, wearing a suit they had never seen before.

CHAPTER FOUR: TRUST FALL

They followed the signal across rooftops, through quiet streets and into a tower that did not appear on any map.

Nova admitted she had hidden something: the signal was responding to Kai, not the city.

“I was afraid you'd leave,” she said. For the first time that night, Kai believed her.

CHAPTER FIVE: THE CHOICE

The tower began to split between two realities. Nova reached for the control panel while Kai held the unstable portal open.

“If we do this, there is no guarantee we'll land in the same place,” Nova warned.

Kai looked at her. “Then we'll find each other again.”

Together, they pressed the glowing symbol. The whole city turned blue.

CHAPTER SIX: BEFORE TWELVE

Morning arrived without a sunrise. The portal was closed, the city was safe, and Nova was still beside Kai.

For one quiet moment, neither of them spoke. They had crossed universes, faced the impossible and chosen to trust each other.

Nova smiled. “So, what happens now?”

Kai was about to answer when a new message appeared across the sky.

UNIVERSE 02 DETECTED.

Then someone knocked on the other side of the portal.
