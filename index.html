📖 The Midnight Signal

A mysterious message. An unfamiliar power. A choice that changes everything.

Chapter One: The Strange Light

The city was unusually quiet that evening. From the roof of an old apartment building, Nova watched the lights flicker across the skyline. Then something blue flashed between two distant buildings.

"That definitely wasn't there a minute ago."

Nova checked the device strapped to their wrist. A strange signal had appeared on its screen, followed by three words: FIND THE SOURCE.

Chapter Two: A Message in the Dark

The signal led Nova through quiet streets and into an abandoned research building. The doors were open, but every screen inside displayed the same symbol: a bright blue web.

"Someone wanted me to find this place."

Before Nova could investigate further, the screens switched off. A voice came from the darkness and called Nova by name.

Chapter Three: The Unexpected Ally

A figure stepped out from behind a broken console. They introduced themselves as Echo, a former researcher who had discovered a way to detect unusual energy between different worlds.

Echo explained that the signal wasn't a warning. It was a request for help. Something was interfering with the connections between worlds.

Chapter Four: The Test

Nova agreed to help, but the first test was not a battle. Echo asked Nova to solve a series of puzzles, each one designed to test patience, observation and careful thinking.

"Power is useful. Knowing when to use it is better."

Chapter Five: A Difficult Choice

The final puzzle revealed two signals. One could restore the connection between worlds immediately. The other could reveal who had caused the disruption in the first place.

Nova paused. Rushing could make things worse. The city needed help, but the truth mattered too.

Chapter Six: Before Sunrise

Nova chose to stabilise the connection first. The blue lights returned to the skyline, one by one. Echo promised that the search for answers would continue tomorrow.

Every hero faces uncertainty. What matters is what they choose to do next.

As the sun rose, Nova looked toward the rooftops. This was not the end of the story. It was the beginning of a much bigger one.
