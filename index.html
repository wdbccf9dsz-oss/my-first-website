CHAPTER ONE â THE CITY THAT FORGOT

At exactly 11:47 p.m., every screen in the city went black. Phones stopped working. Traffic lights froze. The enormous digital clock above Central Station displayed a single symbol: a white spider surrounded by a circle of blue light.

Most people thought it was a power failure. But you noticed something different. Every screen came back on at the same moment, and every screen showed the same sentence: WE REMEMBER WHAT YOU FORGOT.

You had seen that symbol before. It was drawn on a page in an old notebook hidden in your home for years. The notebook belonged to someone whose name your family refused to mention.

CHAPTER TWO â THE ROOFTOP

You followed the signal through empty streets until it led you to an abandoned building overlooking the city. The elevator had no power, so you climbed the stairs. At the top, a window stood open to the cold night air.

A figure waited on the roof. Their suit shimmered with red and blue light, and a thin silver thread stretched from their wrist to the tower across the street.

"You're late," the stranger said. "Who are you?" you asked. They looked toward the skyline. "Someone who tried to save this city once. Someone who failed."

CHAPTER THREE â THE MISSING MINUTE

The stranger explained that a minute had disappeared from every clock in the city. Not just a minute of time, but a memory shared by thousands of people. Nobody could remember what happened at 11:47 p.m. the previous night.

A machine called the Memory Engine sat beneath Central Station. It was designed to store memories safely, but someone had changed its purpose. It was now collecting memories and turning them into energy.

The stranger handed you a small device. A blue light pulsed inside it. "The machine recognises you. I don't know why. But if you want answers, you need to reach its control room."

CHAPTER FOUR â THE GLITCH

The entrance to the underground station was covered in strange digital symbols. As you stepped closer, the symbols rearranged themselves into a face made of static.

"You should have stayed forgotten," it said. The ground shook. Lights burst into life, revealing a tall figure surrounded by broken pieces of glowing code.

The figure called itself The Glitch. It claimed the city was safer without its painful memories. It wanted to remove every mistake, every loss and every difficult truth. You realised that it wasn't trying to destroy the city. It was trying to control what everyone was allowed to remember.

CHAPTER FIVE â THE CHOICE

The Memory Engine filled the room with thousands of tiny lights. Each light represented a memory: a birthday, an apology, a friendship, a mistake, a moment someone wished they could change.

The Glitch offered you a deal. You could erase every painful memory in your own life and never feel that pain again. All you had to do was leave the machine running.

For a moment, you considered it. But you remembered that difficult experiences could also teach people, connect them and help them understand each other. A perfect memory was not the same thing as a perfect life.

CHAPTER SIX â BEFORE THE SUNRISE

The battle began when you refused the offer. The Glitch unleashed waves of red energy, and the entire control room shook. You dodged, fought back and used the blue device to interrupt the machine.

With one final strike, the machine shut down. The lights returned to the city. Memories flowed back to their owners, and the missing minute appeared on every clock.

The stranger stood beside you as the sun rose behind the skyline. "You saved them," they said. You looked at the city below. "We saved them."

The stranger smiled, then disappeared into the morning mist. Your device blinked once more. A new message appeared: UNIVERSE 02 DETECTED.

The city was safe. But somewhere beyond it, another signal was waiting.
